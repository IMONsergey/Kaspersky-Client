## Prompt skeleton

```text
Create a completely new finished A4 portrait editorial report page from scratch
in one ImageGen generation. Referenced archive pages are style references only.
Generate layout, typography, background and all 3D artwork together.

Match the approved Kaspersky visual system: tight editorial grid, high information
density, graphite/dark glass, restrained mint-cyan and subtle violet accents,
premium photoreal 3D, no generic cyber clichés.

Only the text explicitly listed below may be visible. Reproduce every character
and wording exactly. Do not paraphrase, summarize, correct, invent, duplicate,
or add labels, captions, interface text, pseudo-text, or text on 3D objects.
```

- Dark analytical pages: approved pages 03, 07, and the AI section/content pages.
