**Support:** Kaspersky has identified the four cyber shifts expected to have the greatest impact on businesses worldwide through late 2026 and early 2027 — turning them into a practical 90-day action plan.

**CTA:** Get your priorities for the next 90 days

## 02 — The four cyber shifts

**H2:** Understand the four shifts reshaping business risk

**Shifts:**

1. AI accelerating attacks
2. Governance of human and non-human identity
3. SaaS ecosystems and supply chain exposure
4. Attacks on AI, data leakage

**For each shift, the guide identifies:**

- The potential business impact
- The decisions that require executive attention
- The functions to involve
- The actions to initiate within 30, 60 and 90 days
- The evidence leadership requires to assess progress

## 03 — The 90-day agenda

**H2:** From four shifts to one business agenda: Why the next 90 days?

**Lead:** Cyber risk is changing faster than many business controls, leading many cyber strategies to treat everything as “urgent”.

**30 days:** Establish visibility via critical, urgent tasks — What to fund in the first 30 days to block threats.

**60 days:** Strengthen and test once critical tasks are completed — How to strengthen and test at 60 days.

**90 days:** Validate and embed at a more measured pace — How to lower the temperature at 90 days.
