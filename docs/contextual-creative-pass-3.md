## Revision goal

The third pass addresses two weaknesses found in the previous creative pass:

1. abstract 3D objects that did not clearly explain the business context;
2. typography compressed into narrow columns or secondary to illustration.

## Visual principles

- Typography is the primary visual layer.
- Headlines, key questions, statistics and action horizons receive more canvas space.
- Illustrations use simple literal business scenarios: deepfake calls, independent verification, incident-response exercises, employee badges, access gates, supplier outages, checkout and warehouse disruption, API tokens, credential revocation and protected developer environments.
- Avoid decorative portals, monoliths, rings, stacked slabs, generic cyber shields, padlocks, AI brains, circuit-board imagery and fake UI.
- Illustration complexity stays low so the page remains editorial rather than cinematic.

## Text invariant

- Source wording, statistics, punctuation and source grammar are preserved.
- No editorial rewriting or post-generation text replacement.
- Text accidentally invented on illustration props is removed through a separate ImageGen correction pass.
- Illustrations remain textless wherever possible.
